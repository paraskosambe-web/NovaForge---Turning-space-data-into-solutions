# Three-minute demo script · Moon Base preset

## 0:00–0:25 · Set the question

Open the NovaForge landing page. Say: “NASA has a large body of microgravity combustion data, but a search result is not the same as a safety decision. This dashboard keeps the evidence and the inference visible.” Select **Moon Base**.

## 0:25–1:00 · Read the atmosphere carefully

Show the separate O₂ concentration, total pressure and calculated ppO₂ reference cards. Say: “The challenge brief gives at least 30% oxygen and at most 10.2 psi. At exactly those boundary values, ppO₂ calculates to 3.06 psi. Higher concentration alone does not establish higher ppO₂ or a specific risk increase.”

## 1:00–1:35 · Inspect the ranking

Open the dashboard and point to the evidence and inferred columns. Say: “The current PSI summaries don’t provide structured oxygen, pressure or airflow values, so the evidence score is unavailable. The order is an explicitly inferred topic shortlist, not a NASA safety rating.” Open BASS-II or Saffire and show the PSI source link.

## 1:35–2:05 · Compare and trace a real file

Open Compare, select FLEX and FLEX-2 or BASS and BASS-II, and inspect shared metadata and unavailable conditions. Then open PSI-22. Show the downloaded E-FIELD table and explain that the displayed fuel concentration and flames-ignited count are recorded table fields, not safety thresholds.

## 2:05–2:35 · Ask the SI layer

Ask: “Which tests are most relevant to a 30% O₂ lunar habitat?” Show the **DEMO (cached)** badge, source IDs, and the statement that the loaded PSI summaries cannot establish a direct atmosphere match. Then ask for a fabric’s safe oxygen percentage on Mars; point out that SI abstains.

## 2:35–3:00 · Show what PSI does not include

Open Data Gaps. Say: “This PSI query has no LUCI or FM² records. NASA has separately published simulated lunar-g LUCI results, which we cite without mixing them into the PSI export. FM² timing remains unverified from the linked current page.” Close with the disclaimer: “This is an educational research tool, not a certified safety system.”
