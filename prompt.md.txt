# NovaForge: Flame in Freefall Dashboard (NASA Space Apps 2026)

You are a senior full-stack engineer. Build a complete, working, demo-ready web dashboard for the NASA Space Apps 2026 challenge "Flame in Freefall: SI-Powered Fire Safety Insights from Microgravity Combustion". Team: NovaForge ("Turning space data into solutions").

## 1. Goal
NASA has decades of microgravity combustion experiments, but the data in the Physical Sciences Informatics (PSI) repository is hard to find, compare, and interpret. Build an interactive, AI-powered dashboard that SUMMARIZES, RANKS, and INTERPRETS these investigations to deliver fire safety insights for human space exploration: spacecraft, space stations, Moon base, Mars base, and pressurized rovers.

Out of scope: rocket and propellant fire (combustion in rocket engines is effectively independent of gravity).

Target audience: professors, graduate and undergraduate students, aerospace researchers, and aerospace engineers.

## 3. Investigations to cover (minimum)

- Solids flammability: BASS, BASS-II, Saffire-I/II/III (fabrics, plastics)

- Detection: SAME, SAME-R (smoke); DAFT, DAFT-2 (dust, same instrument as SAME)

- Suppression and liquid droplets: FLEX, FLEX-2 (CO2 and helium dilution), CFI (hardware: MDCA in the CIR)

- ACME gaseous-flame experiments (6 total, ACME modular hardware in the CIR), including BRE (materials flammability in still atmospheres as a function of material properties), CFI-G (highest-oxygen microgravity tests, up to 85% O2), and E-FIELD Flames

- MSG / SPICE flow-duct group: BASS, BASS-II, SPICE, SLICE (solid and gaseous fuels, same modular fan-driven flow duct in the Microgravity Science Glovebox)

- Other PSI combustion investigations, ranked lower for fire safety relevance



## 4. Scientific accuracy rules (non-negotiable)

1. NASA PSI is the authoritative source for experimental data.

2. Never generate synthetic experimental measurements and present them as NASA research.

3. Distinguish four value types: measured, calculated, literature-derived, unavailable. Every numeric field in the schema carries: value, unit, type, source_url. The UI shows the type as a badge. A value missing type or source_url must fail Zod validation and break the build.

4. Oxygen analysis must treat three quantities SEPARATELY: oxygen concentration (%), total pressure (psi), and oxygen partial pressure (calculated = fraction x total pressure). Never assume a higher O2 percentage automatically means higher partial pressure or a specific increase in fire risk. Reference scenarios, shown as calculated values with their inputs:

   - Earth sea level: 21% O2 at 14.7 psi, about 3.1 psi ppO2

   - Moon base (expected, per challenge text): 30% O2 or greater at 10.2 psi or less, about 3.1 psi ppO2 at those exact numbers

   - Spacesuit: 100% O2 at 4.3 psi, 4.3 psi ppO2

   The challenge text says the Moon-base O2 concentration is more than 40% higher than Earth's; present that as a concentration comparison only.

5. Every numerical experimental value has a traceable source reference (URL, plus investigation ID and record where available).

6. The ranking must distinguish experimental evidence from inferred relevance (see section 6).

7. Never describe an investigation as proving a material is safe unless the experimental evidence supports that conclusion.

8. Verify claims about lunar gravity, Mars gravity, LUCI, FM2, and E-FIELD Flames against NASA documentation. Known from the challenge text: PSI currently has NO partial-gravity combustion data; LUCI and FM2 are expected to add it; E-FIELD Flames mimics partial gravity via electric-field effects on flame ions; microgravity and normal-gravity results could bound intermediate gravity levels.

9. If NASA data is unavailable, identify the missing information explicitly instead of generating replacement values.

10. Test the AI assistant with questions whose answers are both available and unavailable in the dataset, and document them in TESTING.md.



## 5. Core features

1. Environment-aware ranking. User picks an environment (ISS, spacecraft cabin, Moon base, Mars base, pressurized rover, spacesuit); ranked list with a visible score breakdown. Include a "Moon Base" preset.

2. Investigation explorer. Search and filter by fuel type (solid, gas, liquid droplet), fuel shape and size, O2 concentration, total pressure, airflow speed, hardware, safety category (prevention, detection, suppression), and sensors. Each card shows objectives, fuels, test conditions, variables (what differed vs. stayed the same across tests), measurements, and a source link.

3. Side-by-side comparison of 2 to 4 investigations: similarities, differences, shared hardware, condition ranges, and a chart.

4. Visualizations: condition coverage (O2 % vs. airflow, and ppO2 vs. total pressure with Earth, Moon-base, and spacesuit reference points), a hardware-sharing diagram, a timeline.

5. Predictive-modeling export: CSV and JSON of the actual loaded dataset, with a documented schema and source URLs.

6. Next-gen safety equipment view: detection (SAME, DAFT), suppression (FLEX), materials selection (BASS, Saffire).

7. Earth applications panel: water-mist extinguishers, smoke vs. dust discriminating detectors, firefighter air tanks and protective clothing, linking to the NASA spinoff pages.

8. Data gaps panel: partial gravity, missing fields per investigation, and anything unverified.



## 6. Ranking methodology

Show TWO separate scores for each investigation, never blended into one number:

- Evidence score: how directly the investigation's actual tested conditions match the selected environment (O2 %, total pressure, ppO2, airflow, fuel and material type, relevance to prevention, detection, or suppression). Based only on recorded data.

- Inferred relevance score: how likely the findings apply to the environment based on reasoning (e.g., related hardware or fuel class). Always labeled "inferred".

Weights are editable and documented in /methodology. Show the breakdown and the data fields behind each score.



## 7. AI ("SI") layer

- LLM assistant that (a) writes plain-language summaries per investigation, (b) explains rankings, (c) answers questions such as "Which tests are most relevant to a 30% O2 lunar habitat?", (d) compares investigations.

- Retrieval-augmented: retrieve relevant records first and pass ONLY those to the model. The model may answer only from them, must cite which investigations and records it used, and must say when the data does not support an answer. It must not invent flammability values or safety claims.

- Provider-agnostic wrapper (Anthropic or OpenAI SDK, chosen via env var). Key read from an environment variable, server-side only.

- Demo mode: may only replay AI answers previously generated from the real loaded dataset. Every response is visibly badged "DEMO (cached)" or "LIVE", and the API returns a mode flag.

- Audience toggle: Student (plain language) vs. Researcher/Engineer (technical detail, units).

- Rate-limit /api/ask and keep responses short or streamed.
